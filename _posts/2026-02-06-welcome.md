Welcome to my portfolio! I'm excited to launch this new site as a place to showcase my work and share my thoughts on software development.

## Why I Built This Site

As a developer, I've always believed in the importance of having a professional online presence. This portfolio serves multiple purposes:

- **Showcase Projects**: A curated collection of my best work
- **Share Knowledge**: Blog posts about what I'm learning and building
- **Connect**: A central hub for professional networking

## The Technical Stack

I built this site using a minimalist approach that prioritizes:

- **Performance**: Lightweight, fast-loading pages
- **Maintainability**: Simple markdown-based content management
- **Design**: Clean, professional monochrome aesthetic

The site is powered by [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), which provides:

- Automatic deployment on every push
- Free hosting with HTTPS
- Version control for all content
- No database or server management needed

## What's Next

I'll be regularly updating this site with:

- New project case studies
- Technical blog posts
- Learning resources and tutorials
- Thoughts on software development practices

Stay tuned for more content, and feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect!
